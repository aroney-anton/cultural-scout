# A New Need Taxonomy for the Cultural Scout
*Five independent analyses of the 400-signal corpus, compared, judged, and resolved into one recommendation. 2026-06-10.*

## How this was run

Five agents each read the full 400-signal corpus independently, with no contact between them, each starting from a different methodological prior: pure inductive clustering (A), motivational depth-psychology (B), dialectics, categories as live tensions (C), structural semiotics (D), and working brand strategy (E). Each had to derive 4 to 7 need categories maximizing explained variance, then validate against the same fixed 58-signal evaluation sample (every 7th signal), grading each assignment clean, stretch, or miss. Full reports are in the appendix file.

One caveat before the numbers: each agent graded its own fits, so coverage figures are indicative rather than strictly comparable. The convergence evidence below is more trustworthy than any single percentage.

## The headline finding: five postures, one structure

This is the result that matters most. Despite starting from five different theoretical commitments, the analysts converged on essentially the same seven clusters. When an inductive purist, a depth psychologist, a dialectician, a semiotician, and a strategist all independently carve the same joints, the joints are probably real.

| Cluster | A | B | C | D | E | Convergence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Human provenance / the real | Reality-Footing | REAL | Human Trace | Provenance | Certified Human | 5/5 |
| Bodily togetherness | Live Togetherness | COMMUNION | Presence vs Capture | Co-Presence | Kinship Infrastructure | 5/5 |
| Re-litigating the past | Settling Accounts | RECKONING + CONTINUITY | Settling Accounts | Repaired Record | Overdue Credit | 5/5 |
| Self-authorship | Terms of My Own | SOVEREIGN | Ascent vs Integrity | Self-Possession | Self-Authorship | 5/5 |
| Defended appetite | Felt Aliveness | APPETITE | Appetite vs Management | (in Self-Possession) | Appetite | 5/5 |
| System legibility | Naming the Harm | ORIENTATION | (partial) | Seeing Power Plainly | Naming the Machine | 5/5 |
| Emotional ballast under doom | (folded elsewhere) | (folded elsewhere) | Feeling Through Doom | Ballast | Ballast | 3/5 |

Two further patterns recurred. First, **Status, a pillar of the current framework, nearly vanished in every analysis.** Analyst E put the question sharply: either prestige is genuinely dissolving into credit-repair and room-access, or indie-intellectual editorial is embarrassed by status and the corpus inherits its blind spot. Both readings are useful to your team; neither supports keeping Status as a top-level category for THIS corpus. Second, the same individual signals produced the same startling reframes across analysts who never spoke: the Siri update as a bereavement event (4 of 5), the guestlist-SaaS as productized co-presence rather than exclusivity (4 of 5), the Ozempic essays as grief for desire itself (4 of 5). Independently replicated startles are validated insights, not one analyst's cleverness.

## Scorecard against the three criteria

**Explanatory power** (self-graded, same 58-signal sample):

| Analyst | Clean | Stretch | Miss | Largest category |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B, motivational | **69.0%** | 27.6% | **3.4%** | 22.4% |
| A, inductive | 65.5% | 25.9% | 8.6% | 25.9% |
| C, dialectical | 63.8% | 31.0% | 5.2% | 22.4% |
| E, strategist | 62.1% | 34.5% | 3.4% | 20.7% |
| D, semiotic | 60.3% | 31.0% | 8.6% | 20.7% |

All five beat the incumbent framework's 15% overflow and flat, uninformative distribution. B wins on the numbers, with the lowest miss rate and highest clean rate. E achieves the same miss rate but with a high stretch rate concentrated in one baggy category (its own self-critique says so).

**Startling insight.** C and B produced the sharpest reframes. C's dialectical frame generated the most genuinely reversing reads (hoarding as militant attachment in the same need-family as anti-Ozempic essays; welfare fraud as institutional repair work; withdrawal as the new apex of ambition). B's "grief split" (attachment to a particular lost other vs. the need for things to last in general) is analytically productive. D's structural readings were strong but often re-derived what others reached more directly.

**Intuitive understandability.** E wins decisively. "Certified Human," "Appetite," "Ballast," "Naming the Machine" are explainable to a CMO in one breath and usable as tags in the Scout UI. C's two-pole tension names are insight-rich but awkward as metadata labels. A's names are evocative but wordy. B's single words are clean but abstract (ORIENTATION explains little until defined).

No single analyst wins all three criteria, but the convergence means you don't have to choose an analyst. You choose the consensus structure and the best name and definition for each consensus cluster.

## The recommendation: seven needs plus an honest residue

**1. Certified Human.** The need to trust that what you perceive and consume is real, traceable, and made by a person who meant it. Provenance claims, anti-slop criticism, durational handcraft, labor-time as content, sincerity as method. The most era-defining category and the one most likely to date; review it in two years.

**2. Kinship.** The need for unmediated bodily togetherness, now deliberately built rather than assumed: gatherings, sleepovers, markets, scenes, mutual aid, and the diagnosed failures (phones, automated intimacy). E's insight is the keeper: togetherness has become infrastructure, something engineered on purpose because it no longer happens by default.

**3. Settling Accounts.** The need for a past that has been fairly judged and properly mourned: recantations, canon recovery, restitution, biopic wars, archive rescue, grief given durable form. The corpus's single largest engine in every analysis (21 to 26% everywhere). Includes B's continuity-and-mourning strand; if it ever exceeds a third of tagged signals, split verdict-revision from mourning into two categories.

**4. Self-Authorship.** The need to be the author, not the authored: reclaimed identities, deliberately destroyed careers, gender as practice, refusal of brand-ness, and the newest variant, the right to illegibility, withholding yourself from platform readability as an identity act. C's tension framing is the working definition: recognition without becoming what recognition demands.

**5. Appetite.** The need to feel your own wanting, hunger, pleasure, intensity, against pharmaceutical, algorithmic, and managerial suppression. The weakest validator in every analysis (the eval grid happened to miss its strongest cluster) but the most brief-generative category and the most consistently startling. Keep it, and re-test after the next harvest; it is partly riding one GLP-1 news cycle.

**6. Ballast.** The need to stay emotionally upright inside ongoing collapse: doom as operating register, grief externalized into form, joy and comedy as discipline, refuge aesthetics. This is what's worth saving from the old "Coping," with a crisper boundary: the explicit register must be weight-carrying under crisis, not generic difficulty-management.

**7. Naming the Machine.** The need to see the systems acting on you, named in words you can repeat: illegitimate power, wage theft as default, clipper economies, algorithmic management. Carries a known genre-confound risk (intellectual magazines diagnose systems by trade), so tag it only when the piece's payload is the unmasking itself.

**Plus: unsettled, kept honest and small.** Every analyst found a 3 to 9% residue of formal-play and pure-craft pieces that process no visible need. Do not invent a pseudo-need to mop them up. And put one item on the watch list: a small but coherent more-than-human cluster (ecology, kinship beyond the human, roughly 2 to 3% of corpus) that two analysts independently flagged as homeless. If it grows next run, it becomes category eight.

**Tie-break rules** (the seams every analyst hit): provenance claim vs. recognition claim goes to Certified Human if the audience is buyers/feeds, Settling Accounts if it's the historical record. Reclamation stories go to Self-Authorship when the living subject narrates, Settling Accounts when the record is corrected on someone's behalf. Doom-bonding goes to Kinship when the payload is the room, Ballast when it's the weight.

## What this changes versus the current five

Connection survives, sharpened into Kinship. Agency survives, sharpened into Self-Authorship. Coping shatters productively into Ballast and Naming the Machine, which were always two different needs (carrying the weight vs. seeing the machinery). Vitality narrows into Appetite, which is more specific and more startling. Status dissolves, its real content redistributing into Settling Accounts (credit), Kinship (the room), and Self-Authorship (refusal as the new summit). Certified Human and Settling Accounts are genuinely new top-level needs the old frame had no slot for, and they are the two largest clusters in the corpus.

## Adoption path

This taxonomy describes this corpus, two runs of indie-intellectual editorial. B's caveat belongs in the record: these are the needs of the chattering class processing culture, not necessarily of mass audiences, and one corpus signal (people preferring AI kitsch) cuts against Certified Human's universality. That's a feature for semiotic scoping, where leading-edge discourse is the point, but worth saying out loud to clients.

Practically, in Claude Code: first re-tag the corpus (one session: "re-tag all 400 signals in corpus.json against the new seven-need taxonomy defined in this doc, keeping the old substrate as legacy metadata, and apply the tie-break rules"), then update the site palette and legend (the template's substrate colors and SUBS list), then run the next monthly harvest tagging directly into the new scheme. Keep both taggings side by side for one cycle so you can see where they disagree, the disagreements will be the most interesting signals.

One validation step I'd insist on before committing: spot-check 15 to 20 signals yourself against the seven definitions. The agents converged, but you are the instrument this framework has to feel obvious to, and Jonny's substrate framework presumably has stakeholders who should see the Status finding before it quietly disappears from a live tool.
