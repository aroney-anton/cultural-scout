### 1. `prescription-pleasure` — Prescription Pleasure
**Status guess:** emergent. **Confidence:** medium (argument), low (imagery).
**Register/tension:** Appetite routed through the pharmacy — the Diet pole colonising the Desire pole until they fuse into one ritual. Diagnosis staged as nightlife; dosing as indulgence; hunger reframed as a cost to be managed. The most current expression of the axis, and the one with the biggest visual gap.
**Evidence:**

The only real photographic register this code has.

**Quote:** "Instead of demanding cheaper food, some people are learning how to need less of it." — Khaoula Ghanem, Mille World.
**Visual notes so far:** warm apothecary wood + backlit jars + kraft paper + handwriting (Barmacy) vs sterile acid-collage pharma illustration (Western pieces). Two visual dialects, one code.

- **Vittles, *Sexless Little Carnivores*** — the article's surface is a provenance story: "ancestral," "human-grade," "real meat," anti-UPF pet food. That frame is Certified Human's, and the article's product imagery should NOT anchor an Appetite code. Its genuine Appetite payload is the wildness-fantasy: appetite wanted raw and animal again while actually managed and neutered — "we want real meat and real animal energy" / "Our pets are predators who eat from cans and plastic pouches" (Emily Kenway).
