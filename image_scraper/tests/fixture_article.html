The Ceramicist Making Funeral Urns Shaped Like Toasters

In a converted garage in Ridgewood, Queens, the ceramicist spends her mornings throwing domestic objects that will one day hold human remains. The practice began, she says, after her grandmother's death.

Collectors have started commissioning urns modelled on their own kitchen appliances, a request the artist finds both funny and devastating in equal measure.

Shelves of works in progress. Photo: Jane Critic.

Her next show opens in September and every piece has already sold.
