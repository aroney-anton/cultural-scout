# Analyst A — Inductive Taxonomy of Needs (400-signal corpus)

**Method note.** I read all 400 digests cold, listed recurring preoccupations (~11 proto-clusters: provenance/anti-slop, durational handcraft, gathering, attention hygiene, grief, canon-revision, identity reclamation, appetite/body, political dread/testimony, creative-economy squeeze, refuge/joy), then merged until each cluster named a distinct *need the culture is visibly processing*. Stable at seven.

## 1. The Taxonomy

**1. Reality-Footing**
Signals processing how to know what is real, human-made, and traceable as AI slop, synthetic feeds, and engineered attention dissolve the ground of perception. Includes provenance claims ("made by humans"), anti-slop criticism, attention hygiene, sincerity-as-method, and durational handcraft as proof of the human hand.
*Need: to trust that what I perceive and consume is real, traceable, and humanly made.*
Examples: #054 (certified-not-AI marketing), #329 ("tasteslop"), #279 (origin-literacy as civic virtue).

**2. Live Togetherness**
Signals mourning or rebuilding unmediated co-presence: gatherings, dancefloors, platonic sleepovers, friend-bands, mutual aid, mentorship — plus diagnoses of what kills it (phones as broken social contract, automated intimacy).
*Need: to be bodily together with other people, unmediated.*
Examples: #057 ("the event is the medium"), #340 (platonic sleepovers), #274 (AI-answered texts degrading intimacy).

**3. Settling Accounts with the Past**
The corpus compulsively reopens the past — to grieve it, re-judge it, or recover what was wrongly buried: critic recantations, gendered-backlash reappraisals, canon recovery of overlooked women, biopic fights over who controls a legacy, elegies for closed spaces, memento mori, even product grief.
*Need: a shared past that has been fairly judged and properly mourned.*
Examples: #350 (re-feeling Britney as shared moral debt), #050 (Siri overhaul as grief event), #248 (email campaign reviving an overlooked painter).

**4. Terms of My Own**
Identity, career, heritage, gender, and visibility claimed as self-authored: reclamations (Hannah Montana), deliberate career destruction, gender-as-practice, untranslated titles, anti-brand stances, and control over one's own legibility (phone-refusal as withholding the self from platforms).
*Need: sovereignty over who I am — and over who gets to define or see me.*
Examples: #191 (Miley reclaiming Hannah Montana), #134 (masculinity as learnable practice), #291 (Sherald withdrawing her own show as power move).

**5. Felt Aliveness**
The body and its appetites reasserted against numbing: eating against GLP-1 suppression, dancing, "bad taste" pleasure, joy-as-discipline, slowness-as-deliverable, perception-retraining art — and the flat-affect pieces ("City of Meh," philosophical listlessness) that name the deficit.
*Need: to feel appetite, pleasure, and bodily aliveness in a sedating era.*
Examples: #343 (Girls Who Devour vs. GLP-1s), #229 (post-Ozempic mourning of desire itself), #146 (joy as political discipline).

**6. Naming the Harm**
Witness and moral clarity under illegitimate power: testimony art from war and prison, diagnoses of power-without-legitimacy and "dream logic" governance, the ethics of depicting violence, justice claims made through practice.
*Need: to name what power is doing, and stand morally upright under it.*
Examples: #120 (mere domination), #295 (war childhood as biennial testimony), #400 (Palestinian kitchen as redress).

**7. Room to Exist**
The material squeeze on meaningful life: rent crunch, wage theft, funding collapse, gatekeeping, luxury-by-predatory-debt, student burnout, the economics of remaining an artist or an audience.
*Need: material conditions that make a meaningful life possible at all.*
Examples: #204 (wage theft as industry default), #244 (creative-class rent exodus), #100 (microbudget as forced default).

Rough whole-corpus shares (my read): Settling ~22%, Terms ~17%, Reality-Footing ~16%, Naming ~13%, Togetherness ~12%, Aliveness ~10%, Room ~7%, residual ~3–5%.

# Analyst B — Motivational Taxonomy of the 400-Signal Corpus

**Posture:** every signal was read for the drive, fear, hunger, or wound it is metabolizing — not its topic. Seven engines emerged at the grain this corpus actually exhibits them.

## 1. The Taxonomy

**1. SOVEREIGN — Self-Authorship.**
The drive to seize back the pen: stars destroying and rebuilding their own careers, artists withdrawing their own shows, identities (gender, genre, brand) treated as practices one writes rather than scripts one receives. Includes the newest variant: deliberate *opacity* — refusing to be legible to platforms and power as an act of freedom.
*Need: to be the author, not the authored.*
Examples: #191 (Miley reclaiming Hannah Montana), #291 (Sherald cancelling her own Smithsonian show), #277 (phone-refusal as withholding the self from platform legibility).

**2. REAL — The Hunger for the Real.**
The panic that artifacts, images, and even feelings may be counterfeit, and the corresponding premium on verified human origin: made-by-humans labels, anti-irony sincerity, imperfection-as-proof, handmadeness as moral category. The wound underneath is epistemic: a world filling with synthetic others.
*Need: to know a warm human hand made this.*
Examples: #054 (certified-not-AI as marketing primitive), #329 ("tasteslop"), #222 (handmade anti-AI aesthetic as horror's appeal).

**3. COMMUNION — Unmediated Attachment.**
Touch-starvation metabolized: gatherings as medicine, platonic sleepovers, dead dancefloors mourned, relational labor that cannot be delegated to AI, and attachment so hungry it bonds to dogs, siblings-through-food, and voice assistants. The wound is mass loneliness; the drive is bodies in a room and bonds that hold.
*Need: attachment that cannot be simulated or outsourced.*
Examples: #057 ("the event is the medium" — Howl in the park), #340 (platonic sleepovers as intimacy infrastructure), #274 (AI-answered texts degrading connection).

**4. RECKONING — Moral Repair.**
The culture settling accounts: recantations, restored credit, re-tried verdicts (Britney, Rey, Goetz), testimony from the incarcerated and the displaced, complicity named. The engine is guilt and debt — the need to make the record just before moving on.
*Need: to confess, restore, witness, and re-try old verdicts.*
Examples: #350 (Britney reassessment as shared moral debt), #210 (who gets the credit in fashion), #295 (Ali Eyal's war testimony at the Whitney).

**5. ORIENTATION — Naming the Weather.**
Compulsive coinage and system-diagnosis: dream-logic governance, clipper economies, sinophobic sinophilia, polycrisis sublime. The engine is dread of illegible manipulation — if I can name the system acting on me, it loses some of its grip.
*Need: to see the invisible machinery before it finishes acting on you.*
Examples: #166 (politics conducted in "dream logic"), #285 (the clipper supply chain), #118 ("sinophobic sinophilia").

**6. APPETITE — Felt Aliveness.**
Defense of wanting itself against engineered numbness: hunger vs. GLP-1s, "vibing" unmasked as sedation, kinetic pleasure, joy-as-discipline, bodies designing buildings. The fear underneath is one's own deadness — desire pharmaceutically, algorithmically, and managerially suppressed.
*Need: to feel one's own wanting — hunger, pleasure, intensity.*
Examples: #343 (female appetite vs. the GLP-1 moment), #229 (Ozempic and the felt loss of desire), #235 (Lee Ufan — slowness as bodily deliverable).

**7. CONTINUITY — Lineage & Mourning.**
Holding what churn erases: archives rescued, canons revised and inherited, 22-year devotional constructions, paper replicas of dead gadgets, grief given durable form. The engine is the terror of impermanence — the need for things to last and for losses to be ritually held.
*Need: lineage against churn; mourning against erasure.*
Examples: #088 (22-year hand-built miniature NYC), #376 (saving the Nollywood archive), #385 (hyperreal paper replicas of obsolete tech).

# Analyst C — Dialectical Taxonomy of Need-Tensions

**Method note.** Read as a dialectician: editorial culture writes about a need precisely where it is contested. I looked for the tensions the corpus keeps re-staging across domains (art, food, internet, fashion, politics) rather than static drives. Seven tensions recurred with cross-domain force.

## 1. The Taxonomy

**1. PRESENCE vs. CAPTURE** — The culture wants genuine co-presence and undivided attention while knowing every moment of presence is now harvestable — as content, data, engagement, or a monetizable guestlist. The pieces stage the fight to be *here* without being *taken*.
*Need in one line:* to be genuinely present with others without the moment being converted into someone else's asset.
*Examples:* #057 (the event is the medium), #275 (broken phone social contract), #345 (why gigs aren't fun anymore).

**2. THE HUMAN TRACE (hand vs. machine)** — As AI floods every channel, the corpus negotiates how to live with machines without dissolving into them: made-by-humans as product claim, "tasteslop," automated intimacy, grief for AI companions, slop reclaimed as art material. The pole isn't refusal — it's keeping a verifiable human signature inside machine-saturated life.
*Need:* to remain (and detect) the humanly-made while using the machines.
*Examples:* #054 (made-by-humans marketing), #329 (TASTESLOP), #274 (Apple AI answering texts).

**3. SETTLING ACCOUNTS (verdict vs. repair)** — A huge tranche of the corpus re-litigates the inherited record: who gets a redemption arc, recantations, canon recovery, biopic wars, archive rescue, restitution. The tension: the need to *judge* the past honestly without destroying its *usability* as inheritance.
*Need:* a past one can both condemn and stand on.
*Examples:* #047 (Lena vs. Lindy — who gets redemption), #350 (re-feeling Britney as shared moral debt), #085 (Nigerian modernism reroutes the canon).

**4. APPETITE vs. MANAGEMENT** — The GLP-1/optimization era makes wanting itself contested: hunger, attachment, embodiment, animal aliveness versus pharmaceutical, algorithmic, and moral regimes that manage desire away. The corpus defends the right to want — while admitting wanting is dangerous.
*Need:* to keep desiring in a culture engineering desire away.
*Examples:* #229 (Ozempic, "was it worth it?"), #343 (Girls Who Devour vs. GLP-1s), #396 (buying back a pet's suppressed wildness).

**5. ASCENT vs. INTEGRITY** — Success stories are now told as refusal stories: cancelling your own Smithsonian show, "it wasn't meant to become a brand," anti-productivity at 77, downsizing from TV to indie, gender and genre as scripts to be rewritten. The tension: the need for recognition without becoming what recognition demands.
*Need:* to make it without being made into it.
*Examples:* #291 (Sherald's withdrawal as power), #236 (Vicuña: busyness is not success), #218 (Torishéju's anti-brand credibility).

**6. FEELING THROUGH DOOM (vigil vs. sedation)** — Under polycrisis, the contested ground is affect itself: grief externalized as monsters, comedy as civic medicine, "vibing" named as sedation, the "polycrisis sublime," joy and sincerity as disciplines. The need is to stay feelingly awake to catastrophe without drowning in it — and without anesthetic calm.
*Need:* to keep feeling honestly amid doom, refusing both numbness and panic.
*Examples:* #301 (polycrisis sublime), #034 (comedy as urgent medicine), #106 (grief processed as monster-design).

**7. LOYALTY vs. EXIT (the commons)** — Markets fighting eviction, art schools corporatized, wage theft as default, welfare that works only when rules are bent, "tear it down and start again," home as unfixed. The tension is Hirschman's: collective structures we can neither trust nor live without — repair them, rout around them, or burn them down.
*Need:* something collective that deserves belonging — and the negotiation when it doesn't.
*Examples:* #203 (Ridley Road market fight), #167 (fraud as welfare maintenance), #312 (tear it down and start again).

The defense is discrimination: the seven tensions distribute (max 22.4%), and 5% miss is honest residue, not a hidden eighth category — but a different analyst could draw the poles elsewhere and should be checked against mine.

# Analyst D — Structural-Semiotic Taxonomy of Need

**Method note.** I looked for the binary oppositions each cluster of signals is trying to resolve, then named the need that resolution serves. Seven deep codes organize this corpus: *generated/grown, exposed/withheld, feed/gathering, erased/redeemed, masked/naked power, collapse/equilibrium, assigned/authored.*

## 1. The Taxonomy

**1. PROVENANCE (the Human Trace)**
Signals resolving the opposition *generated vs. grown / sourceless vs. sourced*: the hand, the hour, the origin, and the imperfection as guarantees that a thing is real. In an era of slop, duration and traceability become sacred marks — labor-time itself is the content.
*Need: to know that things come from somewhere — a human hand, real time, a real place.*
Examples: #054 (made-by-humans marketing), #088 (22-year hand-built NYC), #329 (tasteslop), #279 (origin-literacy as civic virtue).

**2. GOVERNED VISIBILITY (Witnessed on My Own Terms)**
Signals working the *exposure/concealment* and *self-representation/being-represented* codes: surveillance as artwork, phone-refusal as withholding oneself from platform legibility, transparency as material, testimony authored by its subject. The problem isn't being seen but who sets the terms.
*Need: sovereignty over the terms of one's own visibility.*
Examples: #067 (your reality is someone's content), #099 (Privacy Index), #277 (phone-free as refusal of legibility), #309 (formerly incarcerated authorship).

**3. CO-PRESENCE (the Gathered Body)**

# Analyst E — Final Report: Seven Needs the Culture Is Processing

**Method note.** I read all 400 entries, clustered on the *need beneath the novelty note* (not topic), drafted eight candidate categories, merged two (grief + doom-coping; testimony + credit-repair), and pressure-tested the survivors against the 58 [EVAL] entries before locking names.

## 1. The Taxonomy

**1. CERTIFIED HUMAN** — The need to locate, verify, and trust what is real: human-made, sincerely meant, traceably sourced, visibly laboured-over. It covers provenance claims ("made by humans"), anti-polish sincerity, durational handcraft, and trusted human curation as the antidote to slop.
*Need in one line: "Prove to me a person made this and meant it."*
Examples: #054 (certified-not-AI as marketing primitive), #218 (anti-brand origin as credibility), #329 (tasteslop — taste as the last moat).

**2. APPETITE** — The need to feel the body's own wanting — hunger, pleasure, movement, sensory intensity — in an era of pharmaceutical suppression, screen disembodiment, and managed desire. The culture is treating desire itself as endangered and worth defending.
*Need in one line: "Let me actually want things, in my actual body."*
Examples: #229 (post-Ozempic "was it worth it?"), #343 (Girls Who Devour vs GLP-1s), #396 (buying back vicarious wildness via pet food).

**3. KINSHIP INFRASTRUCTURE** — The need for real company, deliberately built: rituals, rooms, scenes, mutual-aid systems, and formats that manufacture togetherness on purpose because it no longer happens by default. Covers platonic sleepovers, friend-bands, markets-as-organising, mentorship, and the engineered guestlist.
*Need in one line: "Build me the structures that put me in a room with my people."*
Examples: #340 (platonic sleepovers as intimacy infrastructure), #057 ("the event is the medium"), #305 (what artists build for one another).

**4. SELF-AUTHORSHIP** — The need to control one's own narrative: reclaiming child-star selves, blowing up careers on purpose, refusing genre, gender as learnable practice, withdrawing your own show as a power move. The fight over who gets to narrate a self — including posthumously.
*Need in one line: "I write me — not the platform, the estate, or the algorithm."*
Examples: #191 (Miley reclaiming Hannah Montana), #291 (Sherald cancelling her own Smithsonian show), #194 (career-pivot as deliberate destruction).

**5. OVERDUE CREDIT** — The need to see the record corrected: overlooked elders re-canonized, "minor" crafts taken seriously, testimony entered into evidence, heritage rerouted around Western centres, restitution named as restitution. The corpus's single biggest engine.
*Need in one line: "See what was always there, and say who it belonged to."*
Examples: #296 (Joan Semmel at 93), #248 (email campaign reviving Cynthia Hawkins), #295 (Ali Eyal's war testimony at the Whitney).

**6. BALLAST** — The need to carry grief, doom, and stacked crises without capsizing: mourning pets, products, bands, futures; doom as operating register; comedy and calm as civic medicine. Not solving the unbearable — metabolizing it.
*Need in one line: "Help me hold the weight and keep moving."*
Examples: #301 (the "polycrisis sublime"), #050 (grieving Siri), #246 (dog heaven at the Biennial).

**7. NAMING THE MACHINE** — The need to have opaque systems made legible: power that no longer pretends (Mere Domination), the clipper economy, attention markets, algorithmic management, wage theft as industry default. Comprehension as the precondition of agency.
*Need in one line: "Show me the machinery acting on me, in words I can repeat."*
Examples: #120 (power without legitimating ideology), #285 (the clipper supply chain), #064 (prediction markets gambling on attention).
